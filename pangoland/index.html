Frequently asked questions

What is Pango Land?

Pango Land is a language-learning game where you play as a pangolin. Explore everyday places, meet characters, and complete tasks while practicing a new language.

Which languages can I learn?

Explore French, German, Dutch, Japanese, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese. The app continues to grow with new conversations and learning content.

How do I learn while playing?

Talk to characters, discover objects, and take on missions in places such as restaurants, farms, and supermarkets. Tap unfamiliar words for a translation, and build your vocabulary through the things you do.

Can I play without speaking out loud?

Yes. Conversations include a No-Mic Mode for moments when you can't speak aloud, so you can keep exploring and practicing quietly.

Where can I download it, and is it free?

Pango Land is available on the Apple App Store and Google Play. It is free to download, with optional in-app purchases. Check your store listing for current pricing and device compatibility.
