Frequently asked questions

What kind of game is PangoLand?

PangoLand is a language-learning game that combines an isometric adventure with minigames, conversations, and quizzes. You play as a little pangolin living in a laboratory, your home base for exploring different locations. Along the way, you meet characters, take on quests, and practise your chosen language through conversations and interactive challenges.

How do I learn a language by playing?

Language learning is part of the gameplay. You discover vocabulary through objects and interactions, practise useful phrases in conversations, and put what you’ve learned to the test in minigames and quizzes. Instead of only memorising words in isolation, you use them in the world you’re exploring.

Which languages can I learn?

You can learn Japanese, German, Dutch, French, Spanish, and Mandarin Chinese. For Chinese, both Simplified and Traditional Chinese options are available.

Is PangoLand suitable for beginners?

Yes! Easy content helps you get started with everyday vocabulary and useful phrases. You can practise through minigames, conversations, and quizzes, returning to familiar content as you build confidence.

Can I practise speaking?

Yes! Conversations let you practise speaking with in-game characters in the language you’re learning. You can try out useful words and phrases in everyday situations, such as ordering a coffee, and get more comfortable using the language yourself.

Do I have to speak out loud to play?

No. Conversations have a no-mic mode, so you can keep playing when speaking out loud isn’t an option—whether you’re on the train, in a café, or sharing a quiet room.

How do I progress in the game?

You can complete quests, level up your Pango, and build friendships with the characters you talk to. Your language learning progresses alongside your adventure, with vocabulary, conversations, and challenges to work through.

What are adaptive quizzes?

Adaptive quizzes change as you progress, so you’re not simply repeating the same fixed set of questions. They give you a way to revisit what you’ve learned and put your growing knowledge to the test.
